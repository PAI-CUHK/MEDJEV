# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and technically constructive community. Participants must be respectful of different backgrounds and viewpoints, use inclusive language, accept good-faith correction, and focus criticism on ideas and implementation rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, publication of private information, and deliberate disruption of project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other action appropriate to the situation.

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, and other project spaces.
